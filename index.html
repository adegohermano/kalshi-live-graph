How to read the pick

UP: BTC is above the strike, YES midpoint is at least 55¢, the YES bid/ask spread is 5¢ or less, and more than 2 minutes remain.

DOWN: BTC is below the strike, YES midpoint is at most 45¢, the YES spread is 5¢ or less, and more than 2 minutes remain.

WAIT: Conditions disagree, information is missing, BTC is very close to the strike, the spread is wide, or the market is near expiry. The checklist does not estimate the probability of winning.

Graph tip: Compare BTC’s direction and position relative to the strike with the YES price. A YES price that does not agree with BTC’s position is a reason to be cautious, not proof that the market is wrong.

The UP/DOWN display is a basic rule-based market lean, not individualized financial advice. It does not account for all market conditions, fees, liquidity, or sudden price changes. Verify quotes and contract details before making any decision; trading can result in losses.
