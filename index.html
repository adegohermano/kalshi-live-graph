How to read the lean

UP lean: BTC is above the strike, YES midpoint is at least 55¢, the YES spread is no wider than 5¢, and more than 2 minutes remain.

DOWN lean: BTC is below the strike, YES midpoint is at most 45¢, the NO spread is no wider than 5¢, and more than 2 minutes remain.

WAIT: Conditions disagree, data is missing, the market is near the strike, the spread is wide, or the contract is close to ending. These are simple dashboard rules—not a trading recommendation.

This dashboard displays public market data and a simple rule-based lean. It does not predict outcomes or provide financial advice. Prices can move quickly, data may be delayed, and you can lose money trading.
