How the market read works

YES lean: the checklist requires BTC to be above the detected strike, recent BTC momentum to be positive, the YES midpoint to be at least 55¢, a YES spread of 5¢ or less, fresh data, and more than 2 minutes remaining.

NO lean: the inverse checklist: BTC below the strike, negative recent momentum, YES midpoint at most 45¢, spread no more than 5¢, fresh data, and more than 2 minutes remaining.

WAIT: a required input is missing, stale, near the strike, near expiry, or the checks disagree. This is a conservative display rule—not a statistically validated forecasting model.

This dashboard is informational, not individualized financial advice. It does not calculate a reliable win probability, account for all contract rules, fees, liquidity, or execution price, and cannot prevent losses. Check the market’s official rules and current quotes before making decisions.
